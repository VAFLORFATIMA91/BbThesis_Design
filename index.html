Redeem your points

Points are awarded after proper segregation or recycling is verified. Redeem them for incentives provided by the barangay. Puroks that hit the segregation target can earn community points for every qualified resident. Verified reports of illegal dumping or overflowing garbage earn points too.

How it works

From a single report to a cleaner community.

  1. Step 1

    Report an issue

    Submit a waste concern with a photo and location details.

  2. Step 2

    Track the ticket

    Follow the status: Pending, In Progress, or Resolved.

  3. Step 3

    Segregate and earn

    Earn points once proper segregation or recycling is verified.

  4. Step 4

    Redeem rewards

    Exchange your points for incentives provided by the barangay.
