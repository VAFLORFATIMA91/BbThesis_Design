Proper segregation points: criteria overview

Points are earned by purok. When your purok is perfect on a collection schedule (it complies with every requirement below), each resident of the purok earns +15 points. The next purok closest to perfect earns 10 points per resident, and the rest earn 5 points.

Requirements for a perfect purok

  1. Waste is separated into biodegradable, non-biodegradable and recyclable.
  2. No mixed waste, for example plastics inside food waste.
  3. Bins are covered and not overflowing.
  4. Bins are set out on the scheduled collection day and time.
  5. No illegal dumping found in the purok during the collection.
